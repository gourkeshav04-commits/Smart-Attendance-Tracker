Getting Started

Enter your Student ID / Username and Portal Password on the main dashboard. Click Access Portal and the tracker will fetch your latest attendance data automatically.

No installation required. Smart Attendance Tracker is fully web-based. Just open the page in any modern browser — Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari.

We support all modern browsers: Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Edge 90+, and Safari 14+.

Attendance

Attendance data is fetched live from your college portal each time you log in. If a class was just marked, wait a few minutes and refresh.

The dashboard shows a Bunkable Classes count per subject, calculated so your attendance stays above 75%.

The subject card turns red with a Danger badge and shows exactly how many consecutive classes you must attend to get back above 75%.

AI Predictions

The predictor models different scenarios — attending all remaining classes, attending 80%, or skipping some — and shows projected final percentages for each case.

Predictions are mathematically accurate based on the data you provide. Always update remaining class count when your timetable changes.

Account

No. Your credentials are never stored on any server. They are used only to fetch data during the session and discarded immediately after.

Click the Logout button on the dashboard. Closing the browser tab also ends your session since no data is persisted.

Privacy & Security

The tracker does not collect or transmit any personal data to external servers. All processing happens locally in your browser.

Only your theme preference and any manual corrections you have applied are saved in localStorage.

Troubleshooting

Try these steps in order:

  1. Check your internet connection.
  2. Verify your credentials on the college portal directly.
  3. Clear your browser cache and reload.
  4. Try a different browser.

Log out and log back in to force a fresh fetch. Use the Corrections feature to manually adjust figures until the portal updates.

Hard-refresh with Ctrl + Shift + R (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + R (Mac). If that doesn't help, clear localStorage in Developer Tools.
